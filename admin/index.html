Departments & Group Chats

Super Admins can assign/remove department heads. Department heads can rename only their own group.

Member Applications

Applications do not create accounts. Review the applicant first. Approval provisions a Supabase invitation and places the member into the Pending Verification queue. Department Heads only see applications for their department.

Chat Reports

Review member reports from private and departmental chats. Department heads only see reports concerning members in their own department.

Identity Audit

Approval, verification and access-status events. Department Heads only see events from their department.

Administrators
