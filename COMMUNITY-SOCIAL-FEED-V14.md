# TCC Community v14 — Social Feed

Adds an approved-members-only community feed.

Features:
- General community posts visible to active community members.
- Department-only posts visible only to active members of that department.
- Likes, comments and delete-own-post.
- Real-time-capable tables for posts, likes and comments.
- Server-side checks prevent posting/interacting outside the member's department.
- Mobile-responsive feed cards and comment forms.

Supabase:
Run `supabase/community_social_feed_v14.sql` after the existing Community migrations.
